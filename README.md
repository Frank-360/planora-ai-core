# Planora AI Core

Planora AI Core is an open-source initiative powering the foundational intelligence behind Planora AI, an AI-driven platform designed to improve learning outcomes for children by enabling teachers to deliver high-quality, structured, and engaging lessons at scale.

## Overview

Across many low-resource environments, millions of children face learning gaps due to inconsistent lesson delivery, limited teaching resources, and overburdened educators. Planora AI addresses this challenge by providing an intelligent system that transforms simple inputs into comprehensive, curriculum-aligned lesson notes.

This repository contains the core logic, frameworks, and structures that enable scalable AI-assisted lesson generation.

## Core Components

- **Lesson Structure Templates**  
  Standardized frameworks for generating clear, structured, and pedagogically sound lesson notes.

- **Lesson Generation Logic**  
  AI-driven instruction patterns that convert user inputs (subject, topic, class level) into complete lesson plans.

- **Curriculum Alignment Frameworks (Evolving)**  
  Modular structures designed to support multiple education systems across Africa and beyond.

## How It Works

Planora AI leverages artificial intelligence to process minimal user inputs and generate detailed lesson notes that include:

- Learning objectives  
- Structured lesson content  
- Teaching strategies  
- Interactive classroom activities  
- Evaluation and assessment methods  

The system is designed with a **human-in-the-loop approach**, allowing educators to review, adapt, and contextualize outputs to suit their classroom environments.

## Impact

Planora AI improves foundational learning outcomes by:

- Enhancing lesson clarity and structure  
- Increasing student engagement and comprehension  
- Supporting teachers in low-resource and high-demand environments  
- Promoting consistency in curriculum delivery  

By strengthening teaching capacity, the platform directly contributes to better educational experiences and outcomes for children.

## System Architecture

Planora AI is built on a modular architecture designed for scalability, adaptability, and performance in low-resource environments.

### Core Layers:

1. **User Input Layer**
   - Accepts structured inputs: Subject, Topic, Class Level
   - Designed for simplicity and mobile accessibility

2. **Processing Layer**
   - Transforms user inputs into structured instructional requests
   - Applies predefined lesson frameworks and pedagogical rules

3. **AI Generation Layer**
   - Utilizes large language models to generate lesson content
   - Ensures outputs are structured, age-appropriate, and curriculum-aligned

4. **Validation & Adaptation Layer**
   - Enables human-in-the-loop editing by teachers
   - Supports contextual adaptation for classroom needs

5. **Output Layer**
   - Produces complete lesson notes including objectives, content, activities, and evaluation
   - Optimized for readability and practical classroom use

### Design Principles:
- Low-bandwidth optimization  
- Modular and extensible architecture  
- Curriculum adaptability across regions  
- Human-centered AI interaction  

This architecture ensures reliable performance across diverse educational environments, including underserved communities.

## Impact Metrics Framework

Planora AI is designed to improve learning outcomes for children through measurable improvements in teaching quality and classroom delivery.

### Key Impact Areas:

1. **Teacher Efficiency**
   - Reduction in lesson preparation time (target: 60–70%)
   - Increased frequency of structured lesson delivery

2. **Lesson Quality**
   - Improved clarity, structure, and completeness of lesson notes
   - Consistency in curriculum-aligned teaching

3. **Student Learning Outcomes**
   - Increased student engagement during lessons
   - Improved comprehension and retention of concepts
   - Better performance in classroom assessments

4. **Access & Inclusion**
   - Increased access to quality teaching resources in low-resource environments
   - Support for teachers with limited training or materials

5. **Adoption & Reach**
   - Number of teachers actively using the platform
   - Number of students indirectly impacted through improved teaching

### Data Collection Approach:
- Teacher feedback surveys  
- Usage analytics (lesson generation frequency)  
- Pilot studies in partner schools  
- Qualitative classroom observations  

This framework enables continuous monitoring, evaluation, and improvement of Planora AI’s impact at scale.

## Use Cases Across Africa

Planora AI is designed to be adaptable across diverse educational systems and contexts in Africa.

### 1. Public Schools in Low-Resource Communities
Teachers use Planora AI to generate structured lesson notes despite limited access to textbooks and teaching materials, improving classroom delivery and student understanding.

### 2. Rural and Underserved Areas
With mobile-first and low-bandwidth design, Planora AI supports teachers in remote locations, ensuring children receive quality education regardless of geography.

### 3. Private and Low-Cost Schools
Schools use Planora AI to standardize lesson delivery across classrooms, improving consistency and academic performance.

### 4. Teacher Training Programs
Planora AI is integrated into training initiatives to help new teachers learn how to structure effective lessons and adopt modern teaching practices.

### 5. Multi-Curriculum Environments
The platform can be adapted to support different curricula, including national African systems and international standards such as British and competency-based curricula.

### 6. EdTech and Government Partnerships (Future)
Planora AI can be deployed at scale through partnerships with education ministries, NGOs, and EdTech organizations to improve learning outcomes for millions of children.

These use cases demonstrate the platform’s scalability and relevance across different educational contexts in Africa.
