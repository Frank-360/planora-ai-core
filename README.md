# Planora AI Core

Planora AI Core is an open-source initiative powering the foundational intelligence behind Planora AI, an AI-driven platform designed to improve learning outcomes for children by enabling teachers to deliver high-quality, structured, and engaging lessons at scale.

## Overview

Across many low-resource environments, millions of children face learning gaps due to inconsistent lesson delivery, limited teaching resources, and overburdened educators. Planora AI addresses this challenge by providing an intelligent system that transforms simple inputs into comprehensive, curriculum-aligned lesson notes.

This repository contains the core logic, frameworks, and structures that enable scalable AI-assisted lesson generation.

## Core Components

- **Lesson Structure Templates**  
  Standardized frameworks for generating clear, structured, and pedagogically sound lesson notes.

- **Lesson Generation Logic**  
  AI-driven instruction patterns that convert user inputs (subject, topic, class level) into complete lesson plans.

- **Curriculum Alignment Frameworks (Evolving)**  
  Modular structures designed to support multiple education systems across Africa and beyond.

## How It Works

Planora AI leverages artificial intelligence to process minimal user inputs and generate detailed lesson notes that include:

- Learning objectives  
- Structured lesson content  
- Teaching strategies  
- Interactive classroom activities  
- Evaluation and assessment methods  

The system is designed with a **human-in-the-loop approach**, allowing educators to review, adapt, and contextualize outputs to suit their classroom environments.

## Impact

Planora AI improves foundational learning outcomes by:

- Enhancing lesson clarity and structure  
- Increasing student engagement and comprehension  
- Supporting teachers in low-resource and high-demand environments  
- Promoting consistency in curriculum delivery  

By strengthening teaching capacity, the platform directly contributes to better educational experiences and outcomes for children.

## Accessibility & Design Principles

- **Mobile-first and low-bandwidth optimized**  
- Designed for **underserved and resource-constrained settings**  
- Scalable across diverse educational systems  
- Simple, intuitive, and teacher-friendly
